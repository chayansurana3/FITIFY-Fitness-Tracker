Understanding your result

BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight. For adults, these ranges are commonly used as a general guide:

  • Below the usual range< 18.5
  • Usual range18.5–24.9
  • Above the usual range25–29.9
  • Higher range30+

BMI is only one measure and does not diagnose health or account for every individual difference. For personal guidance, speak with a healthcare professional.
